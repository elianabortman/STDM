# STDM
Spatio-Temporal Analysis and Predictions of Fine Particulate Matter Concentrations in Inner London

Data sourced from the London Air Quality Network, a monitoring system managed by the Environmental Research Group of Imperial College London. 
The network covers most London boroughs, with additional sites in surrounding areas of Essex, Kent, and Surrey. This study collected data from 
monitoring sites in inner London with continuous readings from 2024-2026.

The aim of this study is to:
- Characterise the spatio-temporal structure of daily PM2.5 concentrations across nine Inner London monitoring stations.
- Identify appropriate space-time series models (STARIMA).
- Evaluate model performance using forecasts.
